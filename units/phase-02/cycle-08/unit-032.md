## 1. Mapa de todo lo que sabes hasta ahora (Fase 2)

### Ciclo 5 — Futuro y planes
- *Going to* para planes/intenciones y predicciones con evidencia.
- *Will* para predicciones generales, promesas, ofrecimientos y decisiones espontáneas.
- Presente continuo con valor de futuro para planes confirmados.
- Vocabulario de planes y viajes.

### Ciclo 6 — Present perfect y experiencias
- Present perfect: have/has + participio pasado.
- Ever/never para experiencias de vida.
- Already/yet/just para hablar de tareas recientes.
- Contraste present perfect vs. pasado simple; for/since.

### Ciclo 7 — Modales de consejo, obligación y permiso
- Should/shouldn't para consejos.
- Have to/must para obligación; contraste don't have to (opcional) vs. mustn't (prohibido).
- Can/could para habilidad, permiso y peticiones.
- Vocabulario de salud y trabajo.

### Ciclo 8 — Comparaciones avanzadas y cuantificadores
- The same as, different from, less/fewer.
- Conectores: because, but, although.
- A few/a little (positivo) vs. few/little (negativo); too much/many; enough.
- Vocabulario de viajes, tecnología y emociones.

**Esto es una ampliación enorme de tu capacidad comunicativa** desde el final de la Fase 1: ahora puedes hablar de futuro, experiencias de vida, dar consejos, expresar obligaciones, y conectar ideas con más matiz y naturalidad.

## 2. Texto integrador: todo junto

```text
Next year, I'm going to travel to Europe — I've already booked my flight!
I think it will be an amazing trip, although I'm a little nervous because
I've never traveled alone before.

I have to prepare a lot before the trip. I should learn a few basic phrases
in other languages, and I must remember to bring my passport. I don't have
to bring too much luggage, though — I've learned to pack light.

I've visited many countries in my life, but I've never been to Italy.
I can speak some Italian, but I can't have a full conversation yet.

My colleague has traveled more than me — she's visited over ten countries!
Her experience is very different from mine, although we both love
learning about new cultures.

Have you ever traveled alone? I'd love to hear about your experience.
```

**No traducimos este texto completo** — léelo con calma y comprueba cuánto entiendes por ti misma. Si algo no reconoces, vuelve a la unidad correspondiente.

## 3. 🧠 Repaso mezclado — todos los ciclos de la Fase 2 juntos

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige la forma correcta de futuro

1. Look at those clouds! It ___ (rain) soon.
2. I already have my ticket — I ___ (fly) to Lima next Tuesday.
3. I think the weather ___ (be) nice this weekend.
4. Someone's calling — I ___ (answer) it!

### Ejercicio 2 — Present perfect: completa correctamente

1. I ___ (never/be) to Japan.
2. ___ you ___ (finish) your homework ___? (yet)
3. She ___ (already/eat) dinner.
4. We ___ (live) in this city ___ (for/five years).

### Ejercicio 3 — Elige el modal correcto

1. You ___ see a doctor if you feel worse. (consejo)
2. Passengers ___ fasten their seatbelts. (obligación fuerte/regla)
3. ___ you swim? (habilidad)
4. You ___ smoke in this building — it's against the law. (prohibición)
5. You ___ come if you're busy — it's optional. (opcional)

### Ejercicio 4 — Comparaciones y conectores

1. My new job is ___ (different) my old one.
2. I have ___ (less/fewer) time than before.
3. ___ it was raining, we still went out. (although)
4. I couldn't come ___ I was sick. (because)

### Ejercicio 5 — Cuantificadores

1. I have ___ (a few/a little) friends here — I'm happy about that.
2. There's ___ (too much/too many) noise in this room.
3. Is this bag big ___ (enough) for all my clothes?

### Ejercicio 6 — Corrige los errores

1. "I have visited Paris in 2019." *(revisa: fecha específica)*
2. "She musts finish the report."
3. "You don't have to smoke here — it's dangerous." *(revisa: ¿prohibido u opcional?)*
4. "I have less friends than before." *(revisa: contable o incontable)*
5. "The soup isn't enough hot."

### Ejercicio 7 — Producción libre (el ejercicio más importante de esta unidad)

Escribe un texto de al menos 10-12 frases sobre un plan de viaje futuro (real o imaginario), combinando:

- una forma de futuro (going to, will, o presente continuo);
- present perfect con ever/never o already/yet;
- al menos dos modales del Ciclo 7 (should, have to/must, can/could);
- al menos un conector (because, but, although);
- al menos un cuantificador del Ciclo 8 (a few, too much/many, enough).

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. Look at those clouds! It'**s going to rain** soon.
2. I already have my ticket — I'**m flying** to Lima next Tuesday.
3. I think the weather **will be** nice this weekend.
4. Someone's calling — I'**ll answer** it!

### Ejercicio 2

1. I'**ve never been** to Japan.
2. **Have** you **finished** your homework **yet**?
3. She'**s already eaten** dinner.
4. We'**ve lived** in this city **for five years**.

### Ejercicio 3

1. You **should** see a doctor if you feel worse.
2. Passengers **must** fasten their seatbelts.
3. **Can** you swim?
4. You **mustn't** smoke in this building — it's against the law.
5. You **don't have to** come if you're busy — it's optional.

### Ejercicio 4

1. My new job is **different from** my old one.
2. I have **less** time than before.
3. **Although** it was raining, we still went out.
4. I couldn't come **because** I was sick.

### Ejercicio 5

1. I have **a few** friends here — I'm happy about that.
2. There's **too much** noise in this room.
3. Is this bag big **enough** for all my clothes?

### Ejercicio 6

1. "I **visited** Paris in 2019." (fecha específica → pasado simple)
2. "She **must** finish the report." (must nunca lleva -s)
3. "You **mustn't** smoke here — it's dangerous." (si es peligroso, es prohibición, no opción)
4. "I have **fewer** friends than before." (friends es contable → fewer)
5. "The soup isn't hot **enough**." (enough va después del adjetivo)

### Ejercicio 7

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Aquí tienes un ejemplo de referencia que integra todos los puntos pedidos:

> "Next summer, I'm going to visit my cousins in Spain. I've already bought my ticket, although I haven't packed yet. I've never been to Europe before, so I'm very excited. I should learn a few basic Spanish phrases before I go, even though I already speak Spanish fluently — I mean, I should learn some regional expressions! I have to renew my passport soon because it expires next month. I can speak English well now, but I couldn't have this conversation two years ago. I don't have too much luggage — I've learned to pack light. Although the flight is long, I think it will be a wonderful trip. I have a few friends who have visited Spain before, and they've given me a lot of good advice. I just hope I have enough time to see everything I want to see!"

Compara tu texto con este ejemplo solo como referencia de estructura — tu contenido debe reflejar tus propias ideas.

## 5. ✅ Checklist de cierre de la Fase 2 (A2)

- [ ] Puedo hablar de planes futuros con going to, will y presente continuo.
- [ ] Puedo preguntar y hablar sobre experiencias de vida con present perfect.
- [ ] Puedo usar already, yet y just correctamente.
- [ ] Puedo elegir entre pasado simple y present perfect según el contexto.
- [ ] Puedo dar consejos con should.
- [ ] Puedo expresar obligación (have to/must) y distinguir opcional de prohibido.
- [ ] Puedo hablar de habilidades y hacer peticiones con can/could.
- [ ] Puedo hacer comparaciones más ricas (the same as, different from, less/fewer).
- [ ] Puedo conectar ideas con because, but y although.
- [ ] Puedo usar cuantificadores adicionales (a few, a little, too much, enough).
