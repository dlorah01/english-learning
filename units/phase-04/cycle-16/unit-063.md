## 3. Combinando vocabulario académico/profesional con estructuras del Ciclo 15

Este vocabulario suena especialmente natural cuando se combina con la voz pasiva, discourse markers y hedging que ya dominas:

```text
The research was conducted over a period of six months. The findings
suggest that remote work tends to increase productivity, although
this may vary depending on the industry.

Moreover, the data indicates a significant correlation between
flexible schedules and employee satisfaction. However, further
research is needed to confirm these results.

In conclusion, while the findings are promising, it would be
premature to draw definitive conclusions without additional studies.
```

Traducción:

> La investigación se realizó durante un período de seis meses. Los hallazgos
> sugieren que el trabajo remoto tiende a aumentar la productividad, aunque
> esto puede variar según la industria.
>
> Además, los datos indican una correlación significativa entre
> horarios flexibles y satisfacción del empleado. Sin embargo, se necesita más
> investigación para confirmar estos resultados.
>
> En conclusión, aunque los hallazgos son prometedores, sería
> prematuro sacar conclusiones definitivas sin estudios adicionales.

## 4. Ejemplo en contexto: un informe profesional completo

```text
This report examines the impact of the new training program on
employee performance. The methodology involved surveying 200
employees over a six-month period.

The findings indicate a significant improvement in productivity.
Moreover, employee satisfaction scores increased by 20%. As a
result, the company plans to expand the program company-wide.

However, it should be noted that these results may be influenced
by other factors, such as seasonal trends. Further research would
help clarify these findings.

In conclusion, the data suggests that the training program has had
a positive impact, although additional studies would strengthen
these conclusions. Overall, the initial results are encouraging.
```

**No traducimos este texto completo** — léelo con calma, identificando vocabulario académico/profesional y discourse markers.

## 6. Práctica guiada (con ayuda)

**Frase 1:** La investigación se realizó durante seis meses.
Pista: usa voz pasiva
Respuesta: **The research was conducted over six months.**

**Frase 2:** Los hallazgos sugieren una mejora.
Pista: usa "findings" y "suggest"
Respuesta: **The findings suggest an improvement.**

**Frase 3:** Necesitamos aprovechar esta oportunidad.
Pista: usa "leverage"
Respuesta: **We need to leverage this opportunity.**

## 7. 🧠 Retrieval practice

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa con vocabulario académico

1. The study's ___ suggest a strong correlation between the two variables. (hallazgos)
2. Researchers need to ___ their hypothesis through experiments. (examinar/probar)
3. This journal only publishes ___ articles. (revisados por pares)
4. The ___ of this study involved surveys and interviews. (metodología)

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con vocabulario profesional

1. We need to improve our ___ to reduce waste. (eficiencia)
2. The company is focused on ___ — reducing its environmental impact. (sostenibilidad)
3. We should ___ our existing resources instead of buying new ones. (aprovechar)
4. This process needs to be ___ — it's too complicated. (optimizado)

### Ejercicio 3 — Combina vocabulario académico con discourse markers

Completa: "The research ___ (indicate) positive results. ___, further studies ___ (need) to confirm these ___ (finding)."

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el registro (hazlo más académico/formal)

"We found out that people work better from home. But we're not totally sure why."

### Ejercicio 5 — Repaso mezclado (interleaving: toda la Fase 4)

1. The study ___ (conduct) last year ___ (suggest) that remote work ___ (tend to) increase productivity.
2. If the company ___ (invest) in this research earlier, they ___ (have) more data now.
3. Despite ___ (face) budget constraints, the team ___ (manage) to complete the research.

### Ejercicio 6 — Producción libre

Escribe un breve resumen de investigación o informe profesional (8-10 frases) sobre un tema de tu elección, usando vocabulario académico/profesional, voz pasiva, y discourse markers.

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. The study's **findings** suggest a strong correlation between the two variables.
2. Researchers need to **examine/test** their hypothesis through experiments.
3. This journal only publishes **peer-reviewed** articles.
4. The **methodology** of this study involved surveys and interviews.

### Ejercicio 2

1. We need to improve our **efficiency** to reduce waste.
2. The company is focused on **sustainability** — reducing its environmental impact.
3. We should **leverage** our existing resources instead of buying new ones.
4. This process needs to be **streamlined** — it's too complicated.

### Ejercicio 3

"The research **indicates** positive results. **However/Nevertheless**, further studies **are needed** to confirm these **findings**."

### Ejercicio 4

Ejemplo de versión más formal:

> "The findings indicate that individuals tend to be more productive when working remotely. However, the underlying reasons remain unclear, and further research is needed."

### Ejercicio 5

1. The study **conducted** last year **suggests** that remote work **tends to** increase productivity.
2. If the company **had invested** in this research earlier, they **would have** more data now.
3. Despite **facing** budget constraints, the team **managed** to complete the research.

### Ejercicio 6

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Ejemplo válido:

> "This report examines the effectiveness of a new employee wellness program. The methodology involved surveying 150 employees over a three-month period. The findings indicate a significant reduction in reported stress levels. Moreover, productivity scores increased by 12%. However, it should be noted that these results may be influenced by seasonal factors. As a result, further research would help clarify the program's long-term impact. In conclusion, while the initial findings are encouraging, additional studies are needed before drawing definitive conclusions. Overall, the program appears to be a promising initiative worth expanding."

Revisa: ¿usaste correctamente vocabulario académico/profesional junto con voz pasiva y discourse markers?

## 8. 👂 Mini-listening / pronunciación

- "hypothesis" se pronuncia /haɪˈpɒθəsɪs/ — con el énfasis en la segunda sílaba.
- "methodology" tiene el énfasis en la tercera sílaba: /ˌmeθəˈdɒlədʒi/.
- Practica leyendo en voz alta el informe de la sección 4, con un tono formal y pausado, típico de presentaciones profesionales o académicas.
