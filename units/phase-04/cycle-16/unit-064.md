## 1. Mapa de todo lo que sabes hasta ahora (Fase 4)

### Ciclo 13 — Voz pasiva y registro
- Voz pasiva en todos los tiempos verbales (presente, pasado, continuo, perfect, futuro, modales).
- Registro formal vs. informal: vocabulario, contracciones, phrasal verbs vs. verbos formales.
- Vocabulario profesional básico.

### Ciclo 14 — Third conditional y past perfect
- Past perfect: la acción "antes de la acción anterior".
- Third conditional: hipótesis sobre el pasado.
- Mixed conditionals: combinando tiempos.
- Vocabulario de arrepentimiento y reflexión; should have/shouldn't have.

### Ciclo 15 — Discourse markers y argumentación
- Contraste: however, nevertheless, despite/although.
- Adición: moreover, furthermore, in addition.
- Causa/efecto: therefore, as a result, due to.
- Conclusión: in conclusion, overall.
- Hedging: seem, tend to, might, arguably, sort of.

### Ciclo 16 — Acentos y vocabulario avanzado
- Variedades del inglés: americano, británico, y otros acentos.
- Phrasal verbs de tres partículas y collocations avanzadas.
- Vocabulario académico y profesional.

**Esto representa una transformación real de tu inglés**: de comunicarte con estructuras claras y directas (B1) a poder argumentar, matizar, escribir formalmente, y sonar mucho más como un hablante competente y reflexivo (B2).

## 2. Texto integrador: todo junto

```text
Should companies allow remote work permanently? This question
has been widely debated since the pandemic changed how many of
us work.

Those in favor argue that remote work has had a positive impact
on productivity and work-life balance. Moreover, employees tend
to report higher job satisfaction. As a result, many companies
have already adopted permanent flexible policies.

However, critics point out several challenges. Despite its benefits,
remote work can make collaboration more difficult. Furthermore, some
employees report feeling isolated. It seems that a hybrid approach
might address both sets of concerns.

If companies had implemented better remote infrastructure years ago,
this transition would have been much smoother. Looking back, many
businesses should have invested more in digital tools sooner.

Research conducted on this topic suggests that flexibility, combined
with occasional in-person collaboration, tends to work best for most
organizations. In conclusion, while there's no universal solution,
it appears that a balanced approach is arguably the most sensible
path forward.
```

**No traducimos este texto completo** — léelo con calma. Identifica en él: voz pasiva, registro formal, third conditional, discourse markers de los cuatro tipos, hedging, y vocabulario académico/profesional.

### Ejercicio 8 — Producción libre (el ejercicio más importante de esta unidad)

Escribe un ensayo argumentativo completo (12-15 frases) sobre un tema de tu elección, incluyendo:

- introducción del tema;
- al menos una frase en voz pasiva;
- al menos un discourse marker de cada tipo (contraste, adición, causa/efecto, conclusión);
- al menos una expresión de hedging;
- al menos una estructura condicional (second, third, o mixed);
- vocabulario académico o profesional;
- registro formal (sin contracciones).

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. **The results will be announced tomorrow.**
2. **The project has been completed.**
3. **The form must be submitted by Friday.**

### Ejercicio 2

1. **I discovered the problem.**
2. **We are unable to attend the meeting.**

### Ejercicio 3

1. When I arrived, the meeting **had already started**.
2. If I **had known** about the traffic, I **would have left** earlier.

### Ejercicio 4

1. The plan seemed solid. **However**, it failed.
2. She has great skills. **Moreover/Furthermore**, she has excellent references.
3. Sales decreased. **As a result/Therefore**, they had to cut costs.
4. **In conclusion/Overall**, I believe this is the right approach.

### Ejercicio 5

1. **This seems to be the best solution.**
2. **People tend to be more productive working from home.**

### Ejercicio 6

1. **flat**
2. **methodology**
3. **leverage**

### Ejercicio 7

1. "If it **rains**, we will stay home."
2. "She told **me** that she was tired."
3. "**Despite** the rain, we went out." (o "Although it was raining...")
4. "The company lost money, therefore they **had to reduce costs**."
5. "I have a friend **who** lives in Peru."

### Ejercicio 8

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Aquí tienes un ejemplo de referencia:

> "Should artificial intelligence be used in hiring decisions? This is a topic that has been widely discussed in recent years.
>
> Those in favor argue that AI can be conducted more objectively than human recruiters. Moreover, it can process large volumes of applications quickly. As a result, many companies have already implemented AI screening tools.
>
> However, critics point out significant concerns. Despite its efficiency, AI systems can inherit biases from the data they are trained on. Furthermore, candidates often report feeling that the process lacks a human touch. It seems that these tools, while useful, are not without limitations.
>
> If companies had considered these ethical implications earlier, current regulations might be more comprehensive. Looking back, more research should have been conducted before widespread adoption.
>
> Research suggests that a hybrid approach — combining AI efficiency with human judgment — tends to produce the fairest outcomes. In conclusion, while AI offers valuable tools for hiring, it is arguably most effective when used to support, rather than replace, human decision-making. Overall, careful implementation and ongoing evaluation will be essential moving forward."

Revisa cuidadosamente: ¿incluiste todos los elementos pedidos?
