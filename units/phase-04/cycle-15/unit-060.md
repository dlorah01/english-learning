## 2. Estructura completa de un debate/ensayo argumentativo

1. **Introducción del tema.**
2. **Postura A** — con evidencia, adición (moreover) y hedging para matizar.
3. **Contraste** (however, on the other hand) hacia la postura B.
4. **Postura B** — con su propia evidencia.
5. **Causa/efecto** relevante (therefore, as a result).
6. **Conclusión** (in conclusion, overall) — con una valoración final, posiblemente matizada con hedging.

---

## 3. Ejemplo en contexto: debate estructurado completo

```text
Should schools ban smartphones in the classroom? This is a topic
that generates strong opinions on both sides.

Those who support the ban argue that smartphones are a significant
distraction. Moreover, they can be used for cheating during exams.
As a result, some schools have already implemented strict policies
against phone use.

On the other hand, opponents argue that smartphones can be valuable
educational tools. They provide access to information instantly.
Furthermore, they can help students with disabilities. In addition,
learning to use technology responsibly is arguably an important
life skill.

I would argue that a middle-ground approach seems to be the most
practical option. Schools could allow phones for specific educational
purposes while restricting them during exams and lectures. This
tends to address the concerns of both sides.

In conclusion, while there's no perfect solution, it seems reasonable
to find a balance rather than adopting an absolute policy. Overall,
flexibility combined with clear guidelines is probably the best approach.
```

**No traducimos este texto completo** — léelo con calma, identificando todos los discourse markers y expresiones de hedging.

## 5. 🧠 Retrieval practice — repaso integrador del Ciclo 15

### Ejercicio 1 — Identifica el tipo de discourse marker (contraste, adición, causa/efecto, conclusión)

1. However *(tipo: ___)*
2. Moreover *(tipo: ___)*
3. Therefore *(tipo: ___)*
4. In conclusion *(tipo: ___)*
5. Despite *(tipo: ___)*
6. As a result *(tipo: ___)*

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con el discourse marker apropiado

1. The plan seemed solid. ___, it failed due to unforeseen circumstances.
2. She has great experience. ___, she has excellent references.
3. The company reduced costs. ___, profits increased.
4. ___, I believe this is the right decision.

### Ejercicio 3 — Añade hedging a estas afirmaciones tajantes

1. This policy is wrong. → ___
2. Remote workers are less productive. → ___

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. "Despite it was raining, we went out." → ___
2. "The company lost money, therefore they hired more staff." → ___ *(revisa la lógica)*
3. "This is definitely, absolutely the only correct answer." → ___ *(hazlo menos tajante)*

### Ejercicio 5 — Repaso mezclado (interleaving: toda la Fase 4)

1. The report ___ (submit) yesterday. However, it ___ (seem/contain) some errors that ___ (must/fix).
2. If the company ___ (invest) earlier, it ___ (be) more competitive now. As a result, ___ (they/should/consider) new strategies today.
3. Despite ___ (face) many challenges, the team ___ (achieve) great results.

### Ejercicio 6 — Producción libre (el ejercicio más importante de esta unidad)

Escribe un debate estructurado completo (10-12 frases) sobre un tema de tu elección (trabajo remoto, redes sociales, educación en línea, etc.), presentando dos posturas, usando discourse markers de contraste, adición, causa/efecto, conclusión, y al menos 3 expresiones de hedging.

---

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. contraste
2. adición
3. causa/efecto
4. conclusión
5. contraste
6. causa/efecto

### Ejercicio 2

1. The plan seemed solid. **However**, it failed due to unforeseen circumstances.
2. She has great experience. **Moreover/Furthermore**, she has excellent references.
3. The company reduced costs. **As a result/Consequently**, profits increased.
4. **In conclusion/Overall**, I believe this is the right decision.

### Ejercicio 3

1. **This policy seems to be wrong.** (o "This policy might be wrong.")
2. **Remote workers tend to be somewhat less productive.** (o con "arguably")

### Ejercicio 4

1. "**Despite** the rain, we went out." (despite + sustantivo, no frase completa) o "**Although** it was raining, we went out."
2. "The company lost money, therefore they **had to reduce costs**." (se corrige la lógica: perder dinero normalmente lleva a reducir gastos, no a contratar más)
3. "This is **arguably** the correct answer." (se suaviza con hedging)

### Ejercicio 5

1. The report **was submitted** yesterday. However, it **seems to contain** some errors that **must be fixed**.
2. If the company **had invested** earlier, it **would be** more competitive now. As a result, **they should consider** new strategies today.
3. Despite **facing** many challenges, the team **achieved** great results.

### Ejercicio 6

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Aquí tienes un ejemplo de referencia:

> "Should social media platforms regulate content more strictly? Those who support stricter regulation argue that misinformation spreads quickly online. Moreover, harmful content can affect mental health, especially among young people. As a result, some countries have already introduced new laws.
>
> On the other hand, opponents argue that stricter regulation could limit freedom of speech. Furthermore, deciding what counts as 'harmful' is arguably subjective. In addition, over-regulation might push users to less-regulated platforms.
>
> It seems that finding a balance is the real challenge here. I would argue that transparency and media literacy education tend to be more effective long-term solutions than strict censorship.
>
> In conclusion, while the issue is complex, it appears that a combination of reasonable regulation and public education could address most concerns. Overall, this remains a topic that will likely continue evolving."

Revisa: ¿presentaste ambas posturas, usaste discourse markers variados, y al menos 3 expresiones de hedging?
