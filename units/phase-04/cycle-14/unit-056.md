## 1. Vocabulario: reflexión y arrepentimientos

| Inglés | Español |
|---|---|
| regret | arrepentirse/arrepentimiento |
| in hindsight / looking back | en retrospectiva/mirando atrás |
| mistake | error |
| lesson learned | lección aprendida |
| turning point | punto de inflexión |
| missed opportunity | oportunidad perdida |
| what if...? | ¿qué tal si...? |
| reflect on | reflexionar sobre |

### Expresiones útiles

| Expresión | Significado |
|---|---|
| I should have... | Debería haber... |
| I shouldn't have... | No debería haber... |
| I could have... | Podría haber... |
| Things would be different if... | Las cosas serían diferentes si... |

**Nota gramatical importante:** "should have + participio" (debería haber hecho algo, pero no lo hice) es otra estructura muy útil relacionada con el arrepentimiento, distinta pero complementaria al third conditional:

```text
I should have studied more. (Debería haber estudiado más — no lo hice.)
I shouldn't have said that. (No debería haber dicho eso — lo dije.)
```

## 2. Consolidando todo el Ciclo 14: una reflexión completa

```text
Looking back at my life, there are a few things I would do
differently. If I had studied a second language earlier, I would
be more confident in international settings now.

I remember a turning point in my career. If I hadn't taken that
risk five years ago, I wouldn't be where I am today. At the time,
I was scared, but I'm glad I did it.

There's one missed opportunity I still think about. If I had
applied for that scholarship, I might have studied abroad. I
should have been braver back then.

But overall, I don't have many regrets. Every mistake taught me
something. If I hadn't made those mistakes, I wouldn't have learned
the lessons that shaped who I am today.
```

**No traducimos este texto completo** — léelo con calma, identificando past perfect, third conditional, mixed conditionals, y "should have".

## 4. 🧠 Retrieval practice — repaso integrador del Ciclo 14

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa con past perfect

1. When I arrived, the meeting ___ (already/start).
2. She ___ (never/travel) abroad before that trip.

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con third conditional

1. If I ___ (know) about the traffic, I ___ (leave) earlier.
2. If she ___ (study) harder, she ___ (pass) the exam.

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa con mixed conditional

1. If I ___ (study) medicine, I ___ (be) a doctor now.
2. If he ___ (not/be) so shy, he ___ (speak up) at the meeting yesterday.

### Ejercicio 4 — Completa con should have / shouldn't have

1. I ___ (study) more for the exam. (debería haber)
2. She ___ (say) that to her boss. (no debería haber)

### Ejercicio 5 — Repaso mezclado (interleaving: todo el Ciclo 14 + Fase 4)

1. This report ___ (submit) yesterday. If it ___ (not/submit) on time, we ___ (have) problems now.
2. I ___ (wish) I ___ (learn) English earlier. If I ___ (do) that, I ___ (speak) fluently now.
3. Looking back, I ___ (should/be) more careful with my decisions when I was younger.

### Ejercicio 6 — Producción libre (el ejercicio más importante de esta unidad)

Escribe una reflexión personal (8-10 frases) sobre decisiones importantes de tu vida, usando past perfect, third conditional, mixed conditionals, y al menos una frase con "should have/shouldn't have".

---

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. When I arrived, the meeting **had already started**.
2. She **had never traveled** abroad before that trip.

### Ejercicio 2

1. If I **had known** about the traffic, I **would have left** earlier.
2. If she **had studied** harder, she **would have passed** the exam.

### Ejercicio 3

1. If I **had studied** medicine, I **would be** a doctor now.
2. If he **weren't** so shy, he **would have spoken up** at the meeting yesterday.

### Ejercicio 4

1. I **should have studied** more for the exam.
2. She **shouldn't have said** that to her boss.

### Ejercicio 5

1. This report **was submitted** yesterday. If it **hadn't been submitted** on time, we **would have** problems now.
2. I **wish** I **had learned** English earlier. If I **had done** that, I **would speak** fluently now.
3. Looking back, I **should have been** more careful with my decisions when I was younger.

### Ejercicio 6

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Ejemplo válido:

> "Looking back, there are moments I would have handled differently. If I had traveled more when I was younger, I would have a broader perspective now. I should have taken more risks in my twenties. If I hadn't decided to change careers, I wouldn't have met the people who are now my closest friends. I shouldn't have worried so much about what others thought. If I had trusted myself more, I would have made faster progress. Overall, I don't regret my path, but if I could go back, I would have been kinder to myself along the way."

Revisa: ¿usaste correctamente past perfect, third conditional, mixed conditionals, y should have/shouldn't have?
