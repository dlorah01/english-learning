## 1. El cuarto y último condicional clásico: el pasado que no pasó

Ya conoces el zero, first y second conditional. El **third conditional** completa el sistema, hablando de algo específico: situaciones **hipotéticas sobre el pasado** — cosas que **no pasaron**, y qué habría pasado si hubieran sido diferentes.

```text
If I had studied more, I would have passed the exam.
(No estudié lo suficiente, y no aprobé — pero imaginamos qué habría pasado si hubiera sido diferente.)
```

**Diferencia clave con el second conditional:** el second conditional habla de situaciones hipotéticas en el **presente/futuro** ("si tuviera dinero, viajaría"); el third conditional habla de situaciones hipotéticas que ya **no se pueden cambiar**, porque pertenecen al pasado ("si hubiera tenido dinero, habría viajado" — pero ya pasó, y no lo hice).

---

## 2. La formación del third conditional

### 2.1 El patrón

**If + past perfect, + would have + participio pasado**

```text
If I had studied more, I would have passed the exam.
If she had left earlier, she wouldn't have missed the train.
If we had known about the traffic, we would have left earlier.
```

Traducción:

> Si hubiera estudiado más, habría aprobado el examen.
> Si ella hubiera salido más temprano, no habría perdido el tren.
> Si hubiéramos sabido del tráfico, habríamos salido más temprano.

### 2.2 Forma corta de "would have"

- would have → **would've** (en el habla, suena similar a "would of" — cuidado, "would of" es un error de escritura muy común incluso entre nativos, pero incorrecto; siempre se escribe "would have" o "would've")

---

## 3. Negativo y preguntas

### Negativo

```text
If I hadn't missed the bus, I wouldn't have been late.
If she had known, she wouldn't have said that.
```

### Preguntas

```text
What would you have done if you had been in my position?
If you had known about the problem, would you have said something?
```

---

## 4. Usando el third conditional para expresar arrepentimiento

Este es uno de los usos más comunes y útiles del third conditional en conversación real: expresar que algo del pasado te hace sentir arrepentimiento, o reflexionar sobre decisiones que ya no se pueden cambiar.

```text
I wish I had studied harder. If I had studied harder, I would have
gotten a better job.

I regret not calling her back. If I had called her back, we might
still be friends.
```

**Nota sobre "I wish + past perfect":** esta es otra estructura relacionada, útil para expresar arrepentimiento directamente, sin necesidad de la estructura completa del condicional:

```text
I wish I had studied harder. (Ojalá hubiera estudiado más.)
I wish I hadn't said that. (Ojalá no hubiera dicho eso.)
```

---

## 5. Ejemplo en contexto

```text
Last year, I almost missed a huge opportunity. If I hadn't checked
my email that day, I would have missed the deadline for the job
application. If I had missed it, I wouldn't have gotten this job.

Looking back, I wish I had applied earlier — I was so nervous about
the deadline! If I had been more organized, I wouldn't have had
so much stress.

My colleague once told me that if she had known about the position
sooner, she would have applied too. We often talk about what would
have happened if we had both applied for the same job.
```

Traducción de apoyo:

> El año pasado, casi pierdo una gran oportunidad. Si no hubiera revisado
> mi correo ese día, habría perdido la fecha límite para la solicitud de
> trabajo. Si la hubiera perdido, no habría conseguido este trabajo.
>
> Mirando atrás, ojalá hubiera aplicado antes — ¡estaba tan nerviosa por
> la fecha límite! Si hubiera sido más organizada, no habría tenido
> tanto estrés.
>
> Mi colega una vez me dijo que si hubiera sabido sobre el puesto
> antes, también habría aplicado. A menudo hablamos sobre qué habría
> pasado si ambas hubiéramos aplicado al mismo trabajo.

## 7. Práctica guiada (con ayuda)

**Frase 1:** Si hubiera estudiado más, habría aprobado el examen.
Pista: If + past perfect, + would have + participio
Respuesta: **If I had studied more, I would have passed the exam.**

**Frase 2:** Ojalá hubiera llamado antes.
Pista: I wish + past perfect
Respuesta: **I wish I had called earlier.**

**Frase 3:** Si no hubiera perdido el bus, no habría llegado tarde.
Pista: negativo en ambas partes
Respuesta: **If I hadn't missed the bus, I wouldn't have been late.**

## 8. 🧠 Retrieval practice

### Ejercicio 1 — Completa con third conditional

1. If I ___ (know) about the meeting, I ___ (attend).
2. If she ___ (study) harder, she ___ (pass) the exam.
3. If we ___ (leave) earlier, we ___ (not/miss) the flight.
4. What ___ you ___ (do) if you ___ (be) in that situation?

### Ejercicio 2 — Convierte a "I wish"

1. I didn't study for the exam, and I regret it. → ___
2. She said something rude, and she regrets it. → ___

### Ejercicio 3 — Elige entre second y third conditional

1. If I ___ (have) more money now, I ___ (travel) more. *(situación hipotética presente)*
2. If I ___ (have) more money last year, I ___ (travel) more. *(situación hipotética pasada)*
3. If she ___ (study) harder, she ___ (get) better grades now. *(hipótesis presente)*
4. If she ___ (study) harder, she ___ (pass) that exam last year. *(hipótesis pasada)*

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. "If I would have known, I would have told you." → ___ *(pista: el "if" no lleva "would")*
2. "I wish I studied harder." → ___ *(pista: necesita past perfect)*
3. "If I had study, I would have passed." → ___

### Ejercicio 5 — Repaso mezclado (interleaving: todo el Ciclo 14)

1. When I arrived, the meeting ___ (already/start). If I ___ (leave) home earlier, I ___ (not/be) late.
2. She ___ (tell) me that if she ___ (know) about the problem, she ___ (fix) it earlier.
3. I ___ (wish) I ___ (study) English earlier in my life.

### Ejercicio 6 — Producción libre

Escribe 5 frases con third conditional sobre decisiones o situaciones pasadas que te hubiera gustado que fueran diferentes.

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. If I **had known** about the meeting, I **would have attended**.
2. If she **had studied** harder, she **would have passed** the exam.
3. If we **had left** earlier, we **wouldn't have missed** the flight.
4. What **would** you **have done** if you **had been** in that situation?

### Ejercicio 2

1. **I wish I had studied for the exam.**
2. **She wishes she hadn't said something rude.** (o "I wish I hadn't said that rude thing," si se narra en primera persona)

### Ejercicio 3

1. If I **had** more money now, I **would travel** more. (second conditional)
2. If I **had had** more money last year, I **would have traveled** more. (third conditional)
3. If she **studied** harder, she **would get** better grades now. (second conditional)
4. If she **had studied** harder, she **would have passed** that exam last year. (third conditional)

### Ejercicio 4

1. "If I **had known**, I would have told you." (if siempre en past perfect, sin "would")
2. "I wish I **had studied** harder." (past perfect después de "wish" para arrepentimiento pasado)
3. "If I had **studied**, I would have passed." (participio "studied", no "study")

### Ejercicio 5

1. When I arrived, the meeting **had already started**. If I **had left** home earlier, I **wouldn't have been** late.
2. She **told** me that if she **had known** about the problem, she **would have fixed** it earlier.
3. I **wish** I **had studied** English earlier in my life.

### Ejercicio 6

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Ejemplo válido:

> "If I had known how important English would become, I would have started studying earlier. If I hadn't been so busy last year, I would have traveled more. If I had asked for help sooner, I wouldn't have struggled so much with that project. If she had told me about the opportunity, I would have applied. If we had planned better, we wouldn't have had so many problems."

Revisa: ¿usaste correctamente if + past perfect + would have + participio?

## 9. 👂 Mini-listening / pronunciación

- "hadn't" se pronuncia /ˈhædnt/ — con la "d" y la "n" casi juntas.
