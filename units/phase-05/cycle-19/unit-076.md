## 1. Consolidando todo el Ciclo 19

Este ciclo te ha dado tres herramientas profesionales de alto nivel:

1. **Escritura académica** (Unidad 073): estructura de ensayos, cohesión avanzada.
2. **Negociación y persuasión** (Unidad 074): propuestas, acuerdos, ethos/pathos/logos.
3. **Presentaciones formales** (Unidad 075): apertura, transiciones, manejo de preguntas.

Estas tres habilidades se combinan naturalmente en situaciones profesionales reales: preparas un análisis (escritura académica), lo presentas ante un grupo (presentación formal), y luego negocias los próximos pasos (negociación y persuasión).

---

## 2. Simulacro de presentación completo

Vamos a ver un ejemplo integrador de una presentación profesional que usa las tres habilidades del ciclo, desde la preparación hasta la negociación de próximos pasos.

```text
[APERTURA]
Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here today. I'm excited
to present our analysis of the company's expansion strategy.

[INTRODUCCIÓN AL CONTENIDO — estructura tipo ensayo]
Over the past year, we've seen significant market changes. Today,
I'll argue that expanding into the Southeast Asian market represents
our best growth opportunity. I'll cover three areas: market analysis,
financial projections, and implementation timeline.

[CUERPO — con evidencia y transiciones]
Let's start with the market analysis. Notably, this region has shown
a 25% increase in demand for our products over the past two years.
Turning to financial projections, our data suggests a strong return
on investment within eighteen months.

[PERSUASIÓN — ethos, pathos, logos combinados]
As someone who has analyzed similar expansions for over eight years,
I'm confident in these projections. More importantly, this expansion
would create meaningful opportunities for our team members interested
in international work. The numbers speak for themselves: this is a
sound investment.

[CIERRE]
In conclusion, I believe this expansion aligns with our long-term
goals and offers strong financial returns. Thank you for your
attention. I'd be happy to take any questions.

[PREGUNTA Y NEGOCIACIÓN]
Audience: "What if the timeline needs to be extended?"

Presenter: That's a fair concern. I'd be open to adjusting the
timeline if needed — what if we built in an additional three-month
buffer to account for any unforeseen challenges? I think that would
give us more flexibility while still moving forward with the plan.
```

**No traducimos este texto completo** — léelo con calma, identificando cada sección (apertura, estructura de ensayo, persuasión, cierre, y negociación en la respuesta a la pregunta).

## 4. 🧠 Retrieval practice — repaso integrador del Ciclo 19

### Ejercicio 1 — Identifica la sección de la presentación (apertura, cuerpo/persuasión, cierre, negociación)

1. "Good morning, everyone. Thank you for being here today." *(sección: ___)*
2. "As someone who has analyzed similar expansions for over eight years..." *(sección: ___)*
3. "What if we built in an additional three-month buffer?" *(sección: ___)*
4. "In conclusion, I believe this expansion aligns with our goals." *(sección: ___)*

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con el conector de cohesión apropiado

1. ___, the market has shown strong demand. (Notably)
2. ___ our financial projections, the data is promising. (Turning to)
3. ___, I'd like to emphasize the importance of this decision. (In closing)

### Ejercicio 3 — Escribe una respuesta profesional a esta pregunta difícil del público

"Why should we trust these projections given last year's failed forecast?"

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el registro (hazlo apropiado para una presentación formal)

"So basically, I think this is a good idea, and yeah, that's it."

### Ejercicio 5 — Repaso mezclado (interleaving: toda la Fase 5)

1. She ___ (open) the presentation confidently, although she ___ (admit) she ___ (have) butterflies in her stomach.
2. If the team ___ (prepare) better, they ___ (handle) that difficult question more smoothly.
3. Despite ___ (face) tough questions, the presenter ___ (remain) calm and professional throughout.

### Ejercicio 6 — Producción libre (el ejercicio más importante de esta unidad)

Escribe una presentación profesional completa (12-15 frases) sobre un tema de tu elección, incluyendo: apertura, thesis/argumento central, al menos dos puntos de desarrollo con evidencia, una técnica de persuasión (ethos, pathos o logos), cierre, y una respuesta a una pregunta hipotética que incluya una propuesta de negociación.

---

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. Apertura
2. Cuerpo/persuasión (ethos)
3. Negociación
4. Cierre

### Ejercicio 2

1. **Notably**, the market has shown strong demand.
2. **Turning to** our financial projections, the data is promising.
3. **In closing**, I'd like to emphasize the importance of this decision.

### Ejercicio 3

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Ejemplo válido:

> "That's a fair point, and I appreciate you raising it. This year's methodology incorporates additional data sources that weren't available previously, which we believe significantly improves accuracy. I'm happy to share the detailed methodology after this session."

### Ejercicio 4

Ejemplo de versión más formal:

> "In summary, I believe this represents a strong opportunity for our organization. Thank you for your attention, and I welcome any questions."

### Ejercicio 5

1. She **opened** the presentation confidently, although she **admitted** she **had** butterflies in her stomach.
2. If the team **had prepared** better, they **would have handled** that difficult question more smoothly.
3. Despite **facing** tough questions, the presenter **remained** calm and professional throughout.

### Ejercicio 6

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Aquí tienes un ejemplo de referencia:

> "Good afternoon, everyone. Thank you for joining me today. I'm here to discuss our proposal to implement a four-day work week.
>
> This presentation will argue that a shorter work week can increase both productivity and employee well-being. I'll cover three points: research findings, potential challenges, and our proposed implementation plan.
>
> Firstly, research findings. Notably, companies that have piloted four-day work weeks report productivity levels comparable to, or higher than, traditional schedules. As someone who has studied workplace trends for several years, I find this data compelling.
>
> Turning to potential challenges, some roles require continuous coverage, which we'll need to address through staggered schedules.
>
> In conclusion, I believe this initiative aligns with our commitment to employee well-being while maintaining strong performance. Thank you for your attention. I'd be happy to take any questions.
>
> [Question]: 'How would this affect client-facing roles?'
>
> That's an important consideration. What if we piloted this initially with internal teams before expanding to client-facing roles, allowing us to identify and resolve any issues first?"

Revisa: ¿tu presentación incluye todos los elementos pedidos, integrados de forma natural?

## 5. 👂 Mini-listening / pronunciación
