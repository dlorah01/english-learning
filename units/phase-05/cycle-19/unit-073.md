## 2. Estructura de un ensayo académico completo

### 2.1 Introducción

Una introducción académica sólida generalmente incluye:

1. **Hook** (gancho) — una frase que capta la atención.
2. **Contexto/background** — información general sobre el tema.
3. **Thesis statement** — la idea central/argumento del ensayo, generalmente al final de la introducción.

```text
Climate change is arguably the most pressing issue of our time. (hook)
Over the past decades, scientific evidence has increasingly pointed
to human activity as the primary driver of global warming. (contexto)
This essay will argue that immediate international cooperation is
essential to mitigate the worst effects of climate change. (thesis statement)
```

### 2.2 Párrafos de desarrollo: la "topic sentence"

Cada párrafo del desarrollo debe empezar con una **topic sentence** — una frase que resume la idea principal de ese párrafo, seguida de evidencia, ejemplos y análisis.

```text
One of the primary consequences of climate change is the increase
in extreme weather events. (topic sentence) Studies have shown that
hurricanes and droughts have become more frequent and severe in
recent decades. (evidencia) This trend not only threatens human
lives but also has significant economic implications. (análisis)
```

### 2.3 Conclusión

La conclusión debe **resumir** los puntos principales (sin simplemente repetirlos palabra por palabra) y ofrecer una reflexión final o llamado a la acción.

```text
In conclusion, the evidence overwhelmingly supports the need for
urgent action on climate change. While individual efforts matter,
only coordinated international policy can address a challenge of
this magnitude. The time to act is now.
```

## 3. Recursos de cohesión avanzada

Más allá de los discourse markers básicos (Ciclo 15), la escritura académica de alto nivel usa recursos de cohesión más sutiles:

### 3.1 Referencia (evitar repetición con pronombres y sinónimos)

```text
Climate change poses significant risks. This phenomenon affects
ecosystems worldwide. (evita repetir "climate change" usando "this phenomenon")
```

### 3.2 Sustitución y elipsis

```text
Some argue that regulation is necessary. Others do not believe so.
(en vez de repetir "believe that regulation is necessary")
```

### 3.3 Conectores de secuencia lógica más sofisticados

| Conector | Uso |
|---|---|
| Firstly / Secondly / Finally | Para organizar puntos en una lista formal |
| Notably | Notablemente (para destacar un punto) |
| Specifically | Específicamente |
| In this regard | En este sentido |
| With this in mind | Con esto en mente |

## 4. Ejemplo en contexto: ensayo académico completo

```text
Is remote work here to stay? This question has become increasingly
relevant since the global shift toward flexible working arrangements.
While some organizations have reverted to traditional office models,
this essay argues that remote work represents a lasting transformation
in how we approach employment.

Firstly, remote work offers measurable benefits in terms of employee
well-being. Numerous studies have demonstrated a correlation between
flexible schedules and reduced stress levels. Notably, employees
report greater satisfaction when given autonomy over their working
environment. This finding suggests that rigid office structures may
no longer align with contemporary workforce expectations.

Secondly, from an organizational perspective, remote work can
significantly reduce operational costs. Companies that have adopted
hybrid models report savings on office space and utilities. With
this in mind, many businesses are reconsidering their long-term
real estate strategies.

However, remote work is not without its challenges. Specifically,
maintaining company culture and facilitating spontaneous collaboration
can prove difficult in fully remote settings. In this regard, hybrid
models may offer a practical compromise.

In conclusion, while remote work presents certain obstacles, its
benefits for both employees and organizations suggest that it will
remain a significant feature of the modern workplace. Companies that
adapt thoughtfully to this shift will likely gain a competitive
advantage in attracting talent.
```

**No traducimos este ensayo completo** — léelo con calma, identificando la estructura (introducción, topic sentences, conclusión) y los recursos de cohesión.

## 6. Práctica guiada (con ayuda)

**Frase 1:** Escribe una topic sentence sobre las ventajas de aprender un idioma.
Ejemplo de respuesta: **Learning a new language offers significant cognitive benefits.**

**Frase 2:** Usa "notably" en una frase sobre resultados de investigación.
Ejemplo: **Notably, the study found a strong correlation between sleep and productivity.**

**Frase 3:** Escribe una frase de conclusión usando "In conclusion" y "with this in mind".
Ejemplo: **In conclusion, the evidence is clear. With this in mind, policymakers should prioritize this issue.**

## 7. 🧠 Retrieval practice

### Ejercicio 1 — Identifica la parte del ensayo (hook, contexto, thesis, topic sentence, conclusión)

1. "Climate change is arguably the most pressing issue of our time." *(parte: ___)*
2. "One of the primary consequences of climate change is..." *(parte: ___)*
3. "In conclusion, the evidence overwhelmingly supports..." *(parte: ___)*

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con el conector de cohesión apropiado

1. ___, the study examined three factors. ___, it analyzed the economic impact. (Firstly, Secondly)
2. ___, employees reported higher satisfaction with flexible schedules. (Notably)
3. ___, we should consider long-term implications. (With this in mind)

### Ejercicio 3 — Evita la repetición usando referencia/sustitución

"Remote work has increased. Remote work offers many benefits." → ___

### Ejercicio 4 — Escribe una thesis statement para un ensayo sobre "la tecnología en la educación"

### Ejercicio 5 — Repaso mezclado (interleaving: toda la Fase 5)

1. The essay ___ (argue) that remote work ___ (be) beneficial, although it ___ (acknowledge) certain challenges.
2. If more research ___ (conduct) earlier, the findings ___ (be) more comprehensive now.
3. Despite ___ (face) skepticism, the theory ___ (gain) support over time.

### Ejercicio 6 — Producción libre

Escribe un ensayo corto (10-12 frases) sobre un tema de tu elección, con introducción (hook, contexto, thesis), al menos dos párrafos de desarrollo con topic sentences, y una conclusión, usando recursos de cohesión avanzada.

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. Hook
2. Topic sentence
3. Conclusión

### Ejercicio 2

1. **Firstly**, the study examined three factors. **Secondly**, it analyzed the economic impact.
2. **Notably**, employees reported higher satisfaction with flexible schedules.
3. **With this in mind**, we should consider long-term implications.

### Ejercicio 3

**"Remote work has increased. This phenomenon offers many benefits."** (o "It offers many benefits.")

### Ejercicio 4

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Ejemplo válido:

> "This essay argues that technology, when integrated thoughtfully, can significantly enhance educational outcomes for students of all backgrounds."

### Ejercicio 5

1. The essay **argues** that remote work **is** beneficial, although it **acknowledges** certain challenges.
2. If more research **had been conducted** earlier, the findings **would be** more comprehensive now.
3. Despite **facing** skepticism, the theory **gained** support over time.

### Ejercicio 6

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Ejemplo válido:

> "Should universities require students to take courses outside their major? This debate has persisted for decades in higher education circles. While some argue for strict specialization, this essay argues that a broader curriculum better prepares students for an unpredictable job market.
>
> Firstly, interdisciplinary knowledge fosters creative problem-solving. Notably, many innovative breakthroughs occur at the intersection of different fields. This suggests that narrow specialization may limit students' long-term potential.
>
> Secondly, the modern job market increasingly values adaptable skill sets. With this in mind, universities that offer flexible curricula may better serve their students' future needs.
>
> However, some argue that broader curricula dilute expertise. In this regard, a balanced approach — combining depth in one area with exposure to others — may be the most effective solution.
>
> In conclusion, while specialization has its merits, the evidence suggests that a broader educational approach better equips students for future challenges."

Revisa: ¿tu ensayo incluye todos los elementos estructurales pedidos, con recursos de cohesión avanzada?
