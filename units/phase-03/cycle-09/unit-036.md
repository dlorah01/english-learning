# Unidad 036 — Repaso del Ciclo 9 + vocabulario narrativo

**Fase:** 3 — B1
**Ciclo:** 9 — Pasado continuo y narración
**Unidad:** 036 (última de este ciclo)

---

## 🎯 Objetivos de esta unidad

Al terminar esta unidad podrás:

- Consolidar el pasado continuo, sus contrastes con el pasado simple, y todos los conectores narrativos del ciclo.
- Ampliar vocabulario específico para contar historias y anécdotas.
- Narrar una historia completa, larga y bien estructurada.

## 📋 Conocimientos previos necesarios

Todo el Ciclo 9 (Unidades 033-035).

## 🔁 Repaso rápido (interleaving)

1. Estaba cocinando cuando sonó el teléfono. → ___
2. Antes de salir, revisé el clima. → ___
3. Primero desayuné, luego salí a caminar. → ___

*(Respuestas: I was cooking when the phone rang. / Before leaving, I checked the weather. / First, I had breakfast. Then, I went for a walk.)*

---

## 1. Vocabulario narrativo adicional

Estas palabras y expresiones son extremadamente útiles para dar vida a cualquier historia:

| Inglés | Español |
|---|---|
| suddenly | de repente |
| all of a sudden | de repente (más enfático) |
| eventually | eventualmente/finalmente |
| unexpectedly | inesperadamente |
| luckily / fortunately | por suerte |
| unfortunately | desafortunadamente |
| in the end | al final |
| out of nowhere | de la nada |
| to my surprise | para mi sorpresa |

### Ejemplo de uso

```text
I was walking home when, suddenly, it started to rain heavily.
Luckily, I had an umbrella with me. Unfortunately, my shoes still
got wet. Out of nowhere, a friend appeared and offered to drive me home.
To my surprise, we ended up talking for hours in the car!
```

Traducción:

> Estaba caminando a casa cuando, de repente, empezó a llover fuertemente.
> Por suerte, tenía un paraguas conmigo. Desafortunadamente, mis zapatos igual
> se mojaron. De la nada, apareció un amigo y se ofreció a llevarme a casa.
> ¡Para mi sorpresa, terminamos hablando por horas en el carro!

## 2. Consolidando todo el Ciclo 9: una historia larga y completa

```text
Last month, something unexpected happened to me. I was walking to
the supermarket when I saw a woman drop her wallet. I picked it up
and tried to catch her, but she was walking too fast.

While I was trying to reach her, she turned a corner and disappeared.
Suddenly, I remembered there might be an ID inside the wallet, so I
opened it to find her address.

I decided to walk to her house before returning home. It took me
almost thirty minutes to get there. As soon as I knocked on the door,
a young woman answered — it was her daughter!

I explained everything that had happened, and she was incredibly
grateful. She called her mother immediately. Meanwhile, I waited
outside, feeling proud of myself.

Eventually, the woman arrived, crying with relief. She thanked me
many times before I finally left. In the end, it turned out to be
one of the best days of my month — all because of a small act of
kindness.
```

**No traducimos este texto completo** — esta es tu práctica de lectura del ciclo: lee con calma y comprueba cuánto entiendes por ti misma, identificando cada estructura del Ciclo 9 (pasado continuo, pasado simple, when/while/until/before/after, y los conectores de esta unidad).

## 4. 🧠 Retrieval practice — repaso integrador del Ciclo 9

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige pasado simple o pasado continuo

1. I ___ (walk) to work when I ___ (see) an old friend.
2. While she ___ (cook), the smoke alarm ___ (go off).
3. Suddenly, the lights ___ (go out) while we ___ (watch) a movie.
4. As soon as I ___ (arrive), it ___ (start) to rain.

### Ejercicio 2 — Completa con before, after, until, while o when

1. ___ I left the house, I checked I had my keys.
2. ___ finishing dinner, we watched a movie.
3. She waited ___ he called her back.
4. ___ I was reading, my phone rang.

### Ejercicio 3 — Completa con vocabulario narrativo

1. I was walking calmly when, ___, a dog started barking loudly. (de repente)
2. I lost my phone, but ___, my friend found it. (por suerte)
3. ___, everything worked out fine in the end. (eventualmente)
4. ___ the storm, we still had a great trip. (desafortunadamente — usa una estructura equivalente si no encaja directamente)

### Ejercicio 4 — Ordena la historia con secuenciadores

Une estas frases en una historia coherente usando first, then, after that, meanwhile y finally:

1. I woke up.
2. I had breakfast.
3. I went for a walk.
4. My husband cleaned the house.
5. We had lunch together.

### Ejercicio 5 — Repaso mezclado (interleaving: toda la Fase 3 hasta ahora)

1. I ___ (walk) home when I ___ (see) a beautiful sunset.
2. Before ___ (leave), I ___ (already/pack) my bags.
3. While she ___ (study), her brother ___ (play) video games.

### Ejercicio 6 — Producción libre (el ejercicio más importante de esta unidad)

Escribe una historia de al menos 10-12 frases sobre una experiencia interesante, inesperada o divertida que hayas tenido. Usa: pasado simple, pasado continuo, al menos tres conectores (when, while, as soon as, before, after, until), al menos dos secuenciadores, y al menos dos palabras de vocabulario narrativo (suddenly, luckily, eventually, etc.).

---

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. I **was walking** to work when I **saw** an old friend.
2. While she **was cooking**, the smoke alarm **went off**.
3. Suddenly, the lights **went out** while we **were watching** a movie.
4. As soon as I **arrived**, it **started** to rain.

### Ejercicio 2

1. **Before** I left the house, I checked I had my keys.
2. **After** finishing dinner, we watched a movie.
3. She waited **until** he called her back.
4. **While** I was reading, my phone rang.

### Ejercicio 3

1. I was walking calmly when, **suddenly**, a dog started barking loudly.
2. I lost my phone, but **luckily**, my friend found it.
3. **Eventually**, everything worked out fine in the end.
4. **Unfortunately, the storm happened, but** we still had a great trip. (o de forma más natural: "Although there was a storm, we still had a great trip.")

### Ejercicio 4

**First, I woke up. Then, I had breakfast. After that, I went for a walk. Meanwhile, my husband cleaned the house. Finally, we had lunch together.**

### Ejercicio 5

1. I **was walking** home when I **saw** a beautiful sunset.
2. Before **leaving**, I **had already packed** my bags. *(o simplemente "I already packed my bags")*
3. While she **was studying**, her brother **was playing** video games.

### Ejercicio 6

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Aquí tienes un ejemplo de referencia:

> "Last year, something unexpected happened to me while I was traveling alone. I was waiting for my flight when, suddenly, they announced it was delayed for six hours. At first, I felt frustrated, but eventually, I decided to explore the city. Before leaving the airport, I stored my luggage. As soon as I got outside, I met another traveler with the same problem. We decided to explore together. First, we visited a local market. Then, we had lunch at a small restaurant. While we were eating, we shared stories about our lives. Luckily, we became good friends that day. In the end, the delay turned out to be a blessing in disguise."

Revisa: ¿usaste pasado simple y continuo correctamente, al menos tres conectores, dos secuenciadores, y vocabulario narrativo?

## 5. 👂 Mini-listening / pronunciación

Practica leyendo en voz alta la historia larga de la sección 2, prestando atención al ritmo narrativo completo: pausa después de los secuenciadores, énfasis en las palabras de sorpresa (suddenly, out of nowhere), y fluidez en las combinaciones pasado simple/continuo.

## 6. ✅ Lo que debería quedar aprendido

- [ ] Usar con naturalidad todo el sistema de pasado (simple y continuo) para narrar.
- [ ] Combinar when, while, as soon as, before, after y until con fluidez.
- [ ] Usar secuenciadores para organizar una historia.
- [ ] Usar vocabulario narrativo (suddenly, luckily, eventually...) para dar vida a un relato.

## 🎉 Fin del Ciclo 9

Con esta unidad completas el **Ciclo 9 — Pasado continuo y narración**. Ahora puedes:

- Formar y usar el pasado continuo correctamente.
- Combinar pasado simple y continuo para narrar con matiz.
- Usar múltiples conectores narrativos.
- Contar historias largas, ordenadas y con vocabulario rico.

Este ciclo te ha dado herramientas muy poderosas para la conversación real, ya que contar historias y anécdotas es una de las actividades comunicativas más comunes en cualquier idioma. El **Ciclo 10** te llevará al *present perfect continuous*, un tiempo verbal que combina lo que ya sabes de present perfect (Ciclo 6) con la idea de continuidad (presente y pasado continuo).

---

## 🔁 Lo que se repasará más adelante

- Todo el sistema narrativo del Ciclo 9 se seguirá usando constantemente — es la base de cualquier conversación sobre experiencias pasadas.
- El vocabulario narrativo se ampliará más adelante con expresiones más sofisticadas en fases posteriores.
- Estas estructuras se combinarán con *reported speech* en el Ciclo 12, para reportar historias completas que otras personas contaron.
