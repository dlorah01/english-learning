## 1. Mapa de todo lo que sabes hasta ahora (Fase 3)

### Ciclo 9 — Pasado continuo y narración
- Pasado continuo (was/were + -ing).
- Contraste pasado continuo vs. pasado simple (acciones interrumpidas).
- Conectores narrativos: when, while, as soon as, before, after, until.
- Secuenciadores: first, then, after that, meanwhile, finally.
- Vocabulario narrativo: suddenly, luckily, eventually...

### Ciclo 10 — Present perfect continuous
- Formación: have/has + been + verbo-ing.
- Contraste con present perfect simple (duración vs. resultado).
- Verbos de estado y verbos "camaleón" (think, have, see, taste, look).

### Ciclo 11 — Condicionales
- Zero conditional: hechos generales.
- First conditional: posibilidades reales futuras.
- Second conditional: situaciones hipotéticas + "If I were you".
- Unless, as long as, in case.

### Ciclo 12 — Reported speech, relative clauses, phrasal verbs
- Reported speech con backshift; say vs. tell.
- Relative clauses: who, which, that, where.
- Phrasal verbs y collocations de alta frecuencia.

**Esto representa una ampliación enorme de tu capacidad narrativa y argumentativa** desde el final de la Fase 2: ahora puedes contar historias con matiz, hablar de hipótesis, reportar conversaciones, describir con precisión, y sonar más natural con phrasal verbs.

## 2. Texto integrador: todo junto

```text
Last month, something interesting happened while I was working on
a big project. I was sitting at my desk when my colleague, who I've
known for years, told me she had found a great job opportunity.

She said she was going to apply, and she asked what I would do if
I were in her position. I told her that if I had the chance, I
would definitely go for it.

While we were talking, my boss walked in. He pointed out that we
had a deadline the next day, so we had to get back to work. Later
that day, I found out that my colleague had already sent her application.

If she gets the job, she'll have to move to a different city. Unless
something changes, she'll start in a month. I think it's a place
where she'll be really happy.

I've been thinking about my own career too. If I were braver, I'd
take more risks. Maybe it's time to figure out what I really want.
```

**No traducimos este texto completo** — léelo con calma. Identifica en él: pasado continuo, pasado simple, reported speech, condicionales, relative clauses, y al menos dos phrasal verbs.

## 3. 🧠 Repaso mezclado — todos los ciclos de la Fase 3 juntos

### Ejercicio 1 — Elige pasado simple o continuo

1. I ___ (walk) home when I ___ (see) an old friend.
2. While she ___ (cook), the phone ___ (ring).
3. As soon as I ___ (arrive), it ___ (start) to rain.

### Ejercicio 2 — Present perfect simple o continuous

1. I ___ (live) here for ten years.
2. She ___ (work) all day — she's exhausted.
3. We ___ (visit) five countries together.

### Ejercicio 3 — Identifica el tipo de condicional y complétalo

1. If you ___ (heat) water, it ___ (boil).
2. If it ___ (rain) tomorrow, we ___ (stay) home.
3. If I ___ (have) more time, I ___ (travel) more.

### Ejercicio 4 — Reported speech

1. "I am tired," she said. → ___
2. "I will call you," he said to me. → ___

### Ejercicio 5 — Relative clauses

1. I have a friend ___ lives in Peru.
2. This is the restaurant ___ we met.
3. I bought a book ___ is very interesting.

### Ejercicio 6 — Phrasal verbs y collocations

1. I need to ___ ___ a decision. (tomar)
2. Can you ___ ___ the kids? (recoger)
3. Don't ___ ___! (te rindas)

### Ejercicio 7 — Corrige los errores

1. "If it will rain, we will stay home."
2. "She told that she was tired."
3. "I have a friend which lives in Peru."
4. "If I was you, I would rest."
5. "I have been knowing her for years."

### Ejercicio 8 — Producción libre (el ejercicio más importante de esta unidad)

Escribe un texto de al menos 12-15 frases contando una historia o anécdota, combinando:

- pasado simple y continuo, con al menos un conector narrativo;
- present perfect simple o continuous;
- al menos dos tipos de condicionales;
- al menos una frase en reported speech (say o tell);
- al menos una relative clause;
- al menos dos phrasal verbs o collocations.

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. I **was walking** home when I **saw** an old friend.
2. While she **was cooking**, the phone **rang**.
3. As soon as I **arrived**, it **started** to rain.

### Ejercicio 2

1. I **have lived/have been living** here for ten years.
2. She **has been working** all day — she's exhausted.
3. We **have visited** five countries together.

### Ejercicio 3

1. If you **heat** water, it **boils**. (zero conditional)
2. If it **rains** tomorrow, we **will stay** home. (first conditional)
3. If I **had** more time, I **would travel** more. (second conditional)

### Ejercicio 4

1. She said (that) she **was** tired.
2. He told me (that) he **would call** me.

### Ejercicio 5

1. I have a friend **who** lives in Peru.
2. This is the restaurant **where** we met.
3. I bought a book **which/that** is very interesting.

### Ejercicio 6

1. I need to **make a** decision.
2. Can you **pick up** the kids?
3. Don't **give up**!

### Ejercicio 7

1. "If it **rains**, we will stay home." (if siempre en presente simple)
2. "She told **me** that she was tired." (tell necesita objeto)
3. "I have a friend **who** lives in Peru." (who para personas)
4. "If I **were** you, I would rest." (were, forma tradicional)
5. "I **have known** her for years." (know es verbo de estado, siempre simple)

### Ejercicio 8

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Aquí tienes un ejemplo de referencia que integra todos los puntos pedidos:

> "Last weekend, I was walking in the park when I saw my old neighbor, who I hadn't seen in years. We were talking for a while when it suddenly started to rain. We ran to a café where we used to go as students. While we were having coffee, she told me she had gotten a promotion at work. She said she was very excited but also nervous about the new responsibilities. I told her that if I were her, I would feel proud. She's been working really hard for this opportunity, and I think she deserves it. If she does well in the new role, she'll probably get another promotion next year. Unless something unexpected happens, everything looks great for her. Before we said goodbye, I picked up the bill, and she promised to pay next time. It turned out to be a really nice afternoon, even though we hadn't planned it at all."

Revisa cuidadosamente: ¿incluiste todos los elementos pedidos?

## 🎉 ¡Fin de la Fase 3 — B1!

Felicitaciones por completar **48 unidades** en total, 12 ciclos completos, y todo el nivel B1. Has pasado de una comunicación básica cotidiana (A1-A2) a poder:

- Narrar historias con matiz y fluidez.
- Razonar sobre condiciones e hipótesis.
- Reportar conversaciones.
- Describir con precisión usando cláusulas relativas.
- Sonar más natural con phrasal verbs y collocations.

Este es un logro enorme. La **Fase 4 (B2)** te llevará hacia un inglés más sofisticado: registro (formal/informal), voz pasiva, cuantificadores avanzados, y conectores más elaborados para argumentar y debatir — el tipo de inglés que se necesita en contextos profesionales y académicos más exigentes.
