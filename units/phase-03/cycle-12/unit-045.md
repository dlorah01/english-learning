## 🎯 Objetivos de esta unidad

Al terminar esta unidad podrás:

- Reportar lo que otra persona dijo, usando *reported speech* (estilo indirecto).
- Aplicar el "retroceso" de tiempos verbales (backshift) correctamente.
- Usar *say* y *tell* correctamente, con su diferencia clave de estructura.

## 1. Bienvenida al último ciclo de la Fase 3

Este ciclo cierra B1 con tres herramientas muy útiles: **reported speech** (contar lo que alguien dijo), **relative clauses** (describir con más precisión, sin repetir frases) y tus primeros **phrasal verbs y collocations** — el vocabulario que empieza a sonar realmente natural.

---

## 2. La idea del *reported speech*

Cuando cuentas lo que alguien dijo, tienes dos opciones:

- **Estilo directo**: repites exactamente las palabras de la persona, entre comillas. "I **am** tired," she said. (Estoy cansada, dijo ella.)
- **Estilo indirecto (reported speech)**: cuentas lo que dijo, sin comillas, integrándolo en tu propia frase. She said (that) she **was** tired. (Ella dijo que estaba cansada.)

Fíjate en el cambio: "am" se convirtió en "was". Esto se llama **backshift** (retroceso), y es la regla central de esta unidad.

---

## 3. El backshift: cómo cambian los tiempos verbales

Cuando reportas algo usando un verbo introductorio en pasado (como "said"), los tiempos verbales generalmente "retroceden" un paso:

| Estilo directo | Estilo indirecto (reported speech) |
|---|---|
| presente simple → | pasado simple |
| presente continuo → | pasado continuo |
| pasado simple → | past perfect *(se verá en detalle en una fase posterior; por ahora, es aceptable dejarlo en pasado simple en un nivel B1)* |
| will → | would |
| can → | could |
| have/has (present perfect) → | had (past perfect) |

### Ejemplos

```text
"I am tired," she said. → She said (that) she was tired.
"I work every day," he said. → He said (that) he worked every day.
"I am studying," she said. → She said (that) she was studying.
"I will help you," he said. → He said (that) he would help me.
"I can swim," she said. → She said (that) she could swim.
```

**Nota sobre "that":** la palabra "that" después de "said" es opcional — puedes incluirla o no, sin cambiar el significado. "She said she was tired" y "She said that she was tired" significan exactamente lo mismo.

---

## 4. *Say* vs. *tell*: una diferencia estructural importante

Ambos verbos significan "decir", pero se usan de forma distinta:

### 4.1 *Say*: no necesita un objeto (a quién se le dice)

```text
She said (that) she was tired.
He said he would call later.
```

Si quieres mencionar a quién se le dijo algo con "say", necesitas la preposición "to":

```text
She said to me that she was tired. (menos común, pero correcto)
```

### 4.2 *Tell*: siempre necesita un objeto (a quién se le dice), sin preposición

```text
She told me (that) she was tired.
He told us he would call later.
```

**Patrón:** tell + persona + (that) + frase. No se dice "she told that she was tired" (falta el objeto) ni "she told to me" (con "tell" no se usa "to").

---

## 5. Otros cambios: pronombres y expresiones de tiempo/lugar

Además de los verbos, otros elementos de la frase también suelen cambiar al reportar:

| Estilo directo | Estilo indirecto |
|---|---|
| I | he/she (según quién habla) |
| my | his/her |
| here | there |
| now | then |
| today | that day |
| tomorrow | the next day / the following day |
| yesterday | the day before / the previous day |
| this | that |

### Ejemplo

```text
"I'll finish this today," she said. → She said (that) she would finish that that day. *(suena un poco raro repetir "that" dos veces, así que naturalmente diríamos: "She said she would finish it that day.")*
```

## 6. Ejemplo en contexto

```text
Yesterday, my colleague told me some interesting news. She said she
was going to change jobs next month. She told me she had found a
great opportunity at another company.

I asked her if she was nervous, and she said she was, but she also
said she was excited. She told me she would miss our team, but she
said it was time for a new challenge.
```

Traducción de apoyo:

> Ayer, mi colega me contó una noticia interesante. Dijo que
> iba a cambiar de trabajo el próximo mes. Me dijo que había
> encontrado una gran oportunidad en otra empresa.
>
> Le pregunté si estaba nerviosa, y dijo que sí, pero también
> dijo que estaba emocionada. Me dijo que extrañaría a nuestro equipo, pero
> dijo que era momento de un nuevo desafío.

## 8. Práctica guiada (con ayuda)

**Frase 1:** "Estoy cansada," dijo ella. → Ella dijo que estaba cansada.
Pista: presente → pasado; usa "said"
Respuesta: **She said (that) she was tired.**

**Frase 2:** "Te ayudaré," dijo él. → Él me dijo que me ayudaría.
Pista: usa "told" + objeto
Respuesta: **He told me (that) he would help me.**

**Frase 3:** "Puedo nadar," dijo ella. → Ella dijo que podía nadar.
Pista: can → could
Respuesta: **She said (that) she could swim.**

## 9. 🧠 Retrieval practice

### Ejercicio 1 — Convierte a estilo indirecto usando "said"

1. "I am happy," she said. → ___
2. "I work from home," he said. → ___
3. "I will call you," she said. → ___
4. "I can help you," he said. → ___

### Ejercicio 2 — Convierte usando "told"

1. "I am tired," she said to me. → ___
2. "I will finish it," he said to us. → ___

### Ejercicio 3 — Elige entre say y tell

1. She ___ me she was busy.
2. He ___ (that) he would be late.
3. They ___ us the news yesterday.
4. She ___ she liked the movie.

### Ejercicio 4 — Corrige el error

1. "She told that she was tired." → ___ *(falta el objeto)*
2. "He said me he would call." → ___ *(pista: say no funciona así)*
3. "She said she is tired." → ___ *(pista: revisa el backshift)*

### Ejercicio 5 — Repaso mezclado (interleaving: toda la Fase 3)

1. If I ___ (have) more time, I ___ (learn) a new language. My friend ___ (tell) me she ___ (be/studying) French for a year.
2. She ___ (say) she ___ (be) tired yesterday because she ___ (be/working) all day.
3. If it ___ (rain) tomorrow, I ___ (stay) home — that's what I ___ (tell) my boss this morning.

### Ejercicio 6 — Producción libre

Escribe 5 frases reportando cosas que alguien te dijo recientemente (real o imaginado), usando say y tell correctamente con el backshift.

---

## Solutions

### Ejercicio 1

1. She said (that) she **was** happy.
2. He said (that) he **worked** from home.
3. She said (that) she **would call** me.
4. He said (that) he **could help** me.

### Ejercicio 2

1. She **told me** (that) she **was** tired.
2. He **told us** (that) he **would finish** it.

### Ejercicio 3

1. She **told** me she was busy.
2. He **said** (that) he would be late.
3. They **told** us the news yesterday.
4. She **said** she liked the movie.

### Ejercicio 4

1. "She told **me** that she was tired." (falta el objeto después de "told")
2. "He **told** me he would call." (say no lleva objeto directo sin "to"; con objeto directo se usa "tell")
3. "She said she **was** tired." (backshift: presente → pasado)

### Ejercicio 5

1. If I **had** more time, I **would learn** a new language. My friend **told** me she **had been studying** French for a year. *(o de forma más simple para B1: "she had studied")*
2. She **said** she **was** tired yesterday because she **had been working** all day. *(o "was working")*
3. If it **rains** tomorrow, I **will stay** home — that's what I **told** my boss this morning.

### Ejercicio 6

No hay una única respuesta correcta. Ejemplo válido:

> "My sister told me she was planning a trip to Peru. She said she would travel in December. My friend told me she had gotten a promotion at work. She said she was very happy about it. My mother told me she would visit us next weekend."

Revisa: ¿aplicaste correctamente el backshift y usaste say/tell con la estructura correcta?

## 10. 👂 Mini-listening / pronunciación

- "said" se pronuncia /sed/ — no como se leería en español, sino con una vocal corta similar a "e".
- "told" se pronuncia /toʊld/ — con una vocal larga.
- Practica leyendo en voz alta la sección 6, notando cómo el reported speech le da un tono narrativo natural a contar conversaciones pasadas.
